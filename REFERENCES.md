# Core References and Resources for Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Foundational Textbooks

### Robotics
- Siciliano, B., & Khatib, O. (2016). *Springer Handbook of Robotics* (2nd ed.). Springer. - Comprehensive reference covering all aspects of robotics
- Spong, M. W., Hutchinson, S., & Vidyasagar, M. (2006). *Robot Modeling and Control*. Wiley. - Excellent for kinematics and dynamics
- Craig, J. J. (2005). *Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control* (3rd ed.). Pearson. - Classic introductory text
- Lynch, K. M., & Park, F. C. (2017). *Modern Robotics: Mechanics, Planning, and Control*. Cambridge University Press. - Modern approach with excellent online resources

### Control Systems
- Ogata, K. (2009). *Modern Control Engineering* (5th ed.). Prentice Hall. - Standard control theory reference
- Franklin, G. F., Powell, J. D., & Emami-Naeini, A. (2020). *Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems* (8th ed.). Pearson. - Practical approach to control

### Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning
- Russell, S., & Norvig, P. (2020). *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th ed.). Pearson. - Comprehensive AI textbook
- Sutton, R. S., & Barto, A. G. (2018). *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (2nd ed.). MIT Press. - Essential for RL in robotics

## Key Research Papers

### Humanoid Robotics
- Kajita, S., et al. (2003). "Biped walking pattern generation by using preview control of zero-moment point". *IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation*.
- Hofmann, A., et al. (2007). "A force-actuated dynamic walker with compliant legs". *IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation*.
- Pratt, J., & Krupp, B. (2008). "Capture point: A step toward humanoid push recovery". *IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots*.

### Physical AI
- Pfeifer, R., & Bongard, J. (2006). *How the Body Shapes the Way We Think: A New View of Intelligence*. MIT Press.

### Simulation and Control
- Coumans, E., & Bai, Y. (2016). "Mujoco: A physics engine for model-based control". *IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems*.
- Tedrake, R. (2009). "Underactuated Robotics: Algorithms for Walking, Running, Swimming, Flying, and Manipulation". Course Notes.
