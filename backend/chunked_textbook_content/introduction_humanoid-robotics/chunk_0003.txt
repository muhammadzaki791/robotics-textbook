Why Humanoid Form?

**Human-Centric Environments**: Our world is designed for humans. Door handles, stairs, vehicles, and tools are all optimized for human use. A humanoid robot can potentially use these same interfaces without requiring special adaptations.

**Social Interaction**: Humans are naturally attuned to human-like forms and behaviors. A humanoid robot can leverage our social instincts for more intuitive interaction.

**General Task Capability**: Humanoid form provides versatility. Two arms with hands can manipulate many objects, legs can navigate various terrains, and a head with sensors can perceive the environment from a human perspective.
