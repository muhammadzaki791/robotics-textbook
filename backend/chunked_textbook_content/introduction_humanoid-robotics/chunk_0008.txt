Challenges in Humanoid Design

Creating effective humanoid robots presents several challenges:

**Balance and Locomotion**: Maintaining balance on two legs is surprisingly difficult, especially on uneven terrain or during dynamic movements.

**Dexterity**: Human hands are incredibly sophisticated, with fine motor control and tactile sensitivity that's hard to replicate.

**Computational Complexity**: Coordinating multiple systems in real-time requires significant computational resources.

**Energy Efficiency**: Humanoid robots often consume much more energy than their human counterparts.
