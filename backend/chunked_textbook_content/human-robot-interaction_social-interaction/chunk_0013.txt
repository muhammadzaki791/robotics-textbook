Anthropomorphic Design

The human-like form of humanoid robots creates specific interaction dynamics:

**Theory of Mind**: Humans may attribute mental states to humanoid robots, expecting them to understand and reason about the world.

**Social Categorization**: Humans may categorize humanoid robots as social agents rather than tools, affecting expectations for interaction.

**Uncanny Valley**: When robots look almost but not quite human, they can evoke negative emotional responses.
