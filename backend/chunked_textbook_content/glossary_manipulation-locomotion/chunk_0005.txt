**Prosthetic Control**: Control strategies for robotic prosthetic devices that assist human locomotion or manipulation. **Quadrupedal Locomotion**: Locomotion using four legs, offering stability advantages over bipedal locomotion. **Rapidly-exploring Random Tree (RRT)**: A path planning algorithm that builds a tree of possible paths by randomly exploring the space. **Rhythmic Movement**: Periodic motions that can be used for locomotion or manipulation tasks requiring repetitive motion. **Rolling Gait**: A locomotion mode where the robot moves by rolling, useful for certain terrain types. **Scuffing**: The dragging of feet during walking, which can cause instability in bipedal robots. **Semi-Forced Walking**: A walking mode where the robot uses active control to move the swing leg forward. **Singularity Avoidance**: The process of avoiding configurations where the robot loses degrees of freedom during manipulation. **Singularity Robust Control**: Control strategies that maintain performance near singular configurations. **Slip Detection**: The ability to detect when an object is beginning to slip from the robot's grasp. **Slip Prevention**: Techniques to prevent objects from slipping during manipulation tasks. **Slope Walking**: Walking on inclined surfaces, requiring adjustments to balance and gait parameters. **Squatting**: A low posture where the robot bends its legs, useful for stability or reaching low objects. **Stance Phase**: The phase of walking when the foot is in contact with the ground. **Static Balance**: Balance maintained without motion, typically requiring the center of mass to be within the support polygon. **Static Grasp**: A grasp that holds an object without motion, relying on friction and contact forces. **Step Climbing**: The ability to walk up or down steps or stairs, requiring precise foot placement. **Step Timing**: The precise timing of foot placement during walking, critical for stability. **Stiffness Control**: Control of the mechanical stiffness of the robot's joints or end-effector. **Stumble Recovery**: The process of recovering from a stumble or near-fall during locomotion. **Swing Phase**: The phase of walking when the foot is off the ground and moving forward. **Synchronized Motion**: Coordinated movements of multiple robot parts for complex manipulation or locomotion. **Tactile Feedback**: Sensory information obtained through touch, important for manipulation and locomotion control. **Terrain Adaptation**: The ability to adjust locomotion patterns based on ground conditions.
