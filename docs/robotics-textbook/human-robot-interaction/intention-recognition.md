# Intention Recognition and Prediction

## Understanding Human Intent

For effective collaboration, robots must understand what humans intend to do. This involves:

**Action Recognition**: Identifying what the human is currently doing.

**Goal Inference**: Determining the human's high-level objectives.

**Plan Recognition**: Understanding the sequence of actions the human intends to perform.

## Sensor-Based Intention Recognition

**Visual Analysis**:
- Eye gaze tracking to understand where human attention is focused
- Gesture recognition to interpret communicative movements
- Posture analysis to infer emotional state and intentions

**Audio Analysis**:
- Speech content to understand verbal instructions
- Prosody (tone, rhythm) to infer emotional state
- Environmental sounds to understand context

**Physiological Signals**:
- Heart rate, skin conductance to infer stress or arousal
- Brain-computer interfaces for direct intention reading (emerging technology)

## Machine Learning Approaches

**Supervised Learning**: Train models on labeled examples of human behavior and corresponding intentions.

**Reinforcement Learning**: Learn to predict intentions based on feedback from successful interactions.

**Deep Learning**: Use neural networks to recognize complex patterns in human behavior.

## Predictive Models

**Behavioral Models**: Statistical models of how humans typically behave in specific situations.

**Cognitive Models**: Models based on human psychology and decision-making processes.

**Social Models**: Models that consider social context and norms in predicting behavior.
