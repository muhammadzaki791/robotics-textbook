# Natural Language Interaction

## Speech Recognition

Modern speech recognition systems convert human speech to text:
- **Acoustic Models**: Map audio signals to phonemes
- **Language Models**: Determine the most likely word sequence
- **Context Models**: Use context to improve recognition accuracy

## Natural Language Understanding

Beyond recognizing words, robots must understand meaning:
- **Intent Classification**: Determine the purpose of the human's utterance
- **Entity Extraction**: Identify specific objects, locations, or concepts mentioned
- **Dialogue State Tracking**: Maintain context across multiple turns of conversation

## Natural Language Generation

Robots must generate appropriate responses:
- **Content Planning**: Decide what information to include
- **Surface Realization**: Convert meaning to grammatically correct text
- **Speech Synthesis**: Convert text to natural-sounding speech

## Context-Aware Interaction

Effective language interaction must consider:
- **Spatial Context**: Where objects and people are located
- **Temporal Context**: What has happened previously in the interaction
- **Task Context**: What activity the human and robot are engaged in
- **Social Context**: Cultural norms and relationship between human and robot

## Challenges in HRI Language

**Ambiguity**: Natural language is often ambiguous and requires context resolution.

**Multimodal Integration**: Language must be integrated with other interaction modalities.

**Real-time Processing**: Conversations require rapid response times.

**Personalization**: Interaction should adapt to individual users' communication styles.
