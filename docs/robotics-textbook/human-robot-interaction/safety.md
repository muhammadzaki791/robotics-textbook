# Safety in Human-Robot Interaction

## Risk Assessment

Safety in HRI requires understanding potential risks:
- **Physical Risks**: Injury from robot motion or contact
- **Psychological Risks**: Stress, anxiety, or other negative emotional responses
- **Social Risks**: Violation of social norms or privacy expectations
- **Operational Risks**: System failures that could lead to unsafe conditions

## Safety Standards

**ISO 15066**: Specific safety requirements for collaborative robots working alongside humans.

**ISO 13482**: Safety standards for personal care robots, including humanoid service robots.

**ISO 10218**: Safety requirements for industrial robots (relevant for some HRI scenarios).

## Safety Mechanisms

**Physical Safety**:
- **Speed and Separation Monitoring**: Limit robot speed when humans are nearby
- **Power and Force Limiting**: Design robots with limited force output
- **Safety-Rated Monitored Stop**: Stop robot motion when humans enter safety zones
- **Hand Guiding**: Allow safe direct manipulation of robot motion

**Functional Safety**:
- **Emergency Stop**: Reliable means for humans to stop robot operation
- **Risk Assessment**: Systematic evaluation of potential hazards
- **Safety Functions**: Dedicated safety systems that operate independently of main control

## Collision Avoidance

**Static Collision Avoidance**: Prevent robot from colliding with humans during normal operation.

**Dynamic Collision Avoidance**: React to unexpected human movements during robot motion.

**Compliance Control**: Make robot mechanically compliant to reduce injury potential during contact.
