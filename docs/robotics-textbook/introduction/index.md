# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Define Physical AI and explain its relationship to traditional AI
- Describe the unique challenges and opportunities of humanoid robotics
- Identify key historical milestones in robotics development
- Recognize current applications and future potential of humanoid robots
- Discuss ethical considerations in humanoid robotics development
