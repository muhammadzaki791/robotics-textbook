# Ethical Considerations in Robotics

As humanoid robots become more capable and prevalent, ethical questions become increasingly important:

## Safety and Risk Management

Humanoid robots must operate safely around humans. This requires:
- Robust safety systems to prevent harm
- Fail-safe mechanisms when systems malfunction
- Clear operational boundaries and limitations

## Social Impact

The deployment of humanoid robots raises questions about:
- Job displacement in service industries
- Human relationships and social interaction
- Dependency on robotic systems

## Privacy and Autonomy

Humanoid robots often include cameras, microphones, and other sensors, raising privacy concerns:
- Data collection and storage practices
- Consent for interaction
- Autonomy and decision-making authority

## Human Dignity

There are concerns about:
- How humanoid robots might affect human self-worth
- Appropriate applications for humanoid robots
- Maintaining human agency in an automated world

## Exercises

1. **Research Exercise**: Find three examples of humanoid robots currently in development or deployment. Compare their designs and intended applications. What human-like features do they share, and what makes each unique?

2. **Design Thinking**: If you were to design a humanoid robot for a specific task (e.g., helping in a kitchen, assisting in a library, or working in a factory), what human-like features would be most important? Which could be simplified or omitted?

3. **Ethics Discussion**: Consider a humanoid robot designed to provide companionship for elderly individuals. What ethical considerations should designers address? How might such robots affect human relationships and social structures?
