# Humanoid Robot Design and Embodiment

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Understand the mechanical design principles specific to humanoid robots
- Compare different actuator technologies and their applications
- Analyze power systems and energy management for humanoid robots
- Explain the principles of biomimicry in humanoid design
- Evaluate compliance and safety considerations in humanoid robot design
