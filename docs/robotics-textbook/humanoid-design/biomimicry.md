# Biomimicry in Design

## Human Anatomy Inspiration

**Musculoskeletal System**: Understanding human structure for robotic design.

**Range of Motion**: Matching human joint ranges where beneficial.

**Proportional Design**: Human-like proportions for environmental compatibility.

## Biological Actuation

**Muscle-like Actuators**: Pneumatic muscles and other bio-inspired actuators.

**Tendon Systems**: Cable-driven systems mimicking human tendons.

**Antagonistic Pairs**: Opposing actuators like human muscle pairs.

## Sensory Systems

**Human Vision**: Camera systems designed to mimic human visual capabilities.

**Balance Systems**: Mimicking the human vestibular system.

**Tactile Sensing**: Bio-inspired tactile sensor arrays.

## Locomotion Principles

**Human Walking**: Understanding human gait for robotic walking.

**Balance Strategies**: Mimicking human balance recovery mechanisms.

**Energy Efficiency**: Learning from human energy-efficient movement patterns.
