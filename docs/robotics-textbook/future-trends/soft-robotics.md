# Soft Robotics

## Principles of Soft Robotics

Soft robotics represents a paradigm shift from rigid to compliant robotic systems:
- **Inherent Safety**: Natural compliance reduces injury risk
- **Adaptability**: Conform to irregular surfaces and objects
- **Simplicity**: Often simpler control than rigid systems
- **Bio-inspiration**: Mimicking soft biological systems

## Soft Actuation Technologies

**Pneumatic Networks**: Networks of air chambers for complex movements.
- **Fiber-Reinforced Actuators**: Combining soft materials with reinforcing fibers
- **Braided Pneumatic Muscles**: Contractile actuators inspired by biological muscles
- **Fluidic Elastomers**: Soft actuators using fluid pressure

**Hydraulic Soft Actuators**: Using liquids instead of air for actuation.
- Advantages: Incompressible, precise control, high force density
- Challenges: Sealing, weight, complexity

**Chemical Actuation**: Using chemical reactions for movement.
- **Artificial Muscles**: Biochemical processes for actuation
- **Self-Propulsion**: Chemical reactions for autonomous movement
- **Challenges**: Controllability, sustainability, safety

## Soft Sensing and Control

**Soft Sensors**: Flexible sensors integrated into soft robotic systems.
- **Capacitive Sensors**: Measuring deformation and contact
- **Optical Sensors**: Using light transmission changes
- **Resistive Sensors**: Measuring pressure and strain

**Control Challenges**: Soft systems require different control approaches.
- **Model Uncertainty**: Difficulty modeling highly deformable systems
- **Distributed Control**: Managing many degrees of freedom
- **Learning-Based Control**: Adapting to system changes
