# Collective and Swarm Robotics

## Principles of Collective Behavior

**Emergent Behavior**: Complex group behavior from simple individual rules.
- **Self-Organization**: Spontaneous formation of patterns
- **Distributed Intelligence**: Intelligence emerging from interactions
- **Scalability**: Performance scaling with group size

## Swarm Intelligence Applications

**Coordination**: Multiple robots working together.
- **Consensus Algorithms**: Reaching agreement without central control
- **Task Allocation**: Distributing work among robots
- **Formation Control**: Maintaining desired group configurations

**Collective Manipulation**: Multiple robots manipulating objects together.
- **Cooperative Transport**: Moving large objects together
- **Assembly**: Collaborative construction tasks
- **Adaptive Structures**: Robots forming functional structures

## Human-Swarm Interaction

**Mixed Groups**: Humans working with robot swarms.
- **Supervision**: Managing large numbers of robots
- **Communication**: Effective interfaces for swarm control
- **Trust**: Ensuring reliable swarm behavior

**Bio-Hybrid Systems**: Combining biological and artificial agents.
- **Animal-Robot Interaction**: Robots working with animals
- **Human-Robot Groups**: Mixed human-robot teams
- **Applications**: Search and rescue, environmental monitoring
