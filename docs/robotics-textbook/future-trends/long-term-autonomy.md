# Long-Term Autonomy

## Self-Sufficiency

**Energy Autonomy**: Robots that can operate independently for extended periods.
- **Energy Harvesting**: Collecting energy from the environment
- **Efficient Operation**: Minimizing energy consumption
- **Recharging Systems**: Autonomous refueling and recharging

**Maintenance Autonomy**: Robots that can maintain themselves.
- **Self-Diagnosis**: Detecting and identifying problems
- **Self-Repair**: Fixing minor issues autonomously
- **Self-Calibration**: Maintaining sensor and actuator accuracy

## Self-Improvement Systems

**Adaptive Architecture**: Robots that can modify their own structure.
- **Modular Robots**: Robots that can reconfigure themselves
- **Self-Assembly**: Robots that can build themselves
- **Self-Construction**: Robots that can build other robots

**Evolutionary Robotics**: Robots that evolve their capabilities.
- **Morphological Evolution**: Changing physical structure over time
- **Behavioral Evolution**: Evolving better behaviors through selection
- **Applications**: Adapting to unknown environments

## Self-Organization

**Autonomous Development**: Robots that develop capabilities over time.
- **Developmental Learning**: Learning in stages like biological development
- **Curriculum Learning**: Progressing from simple to complex tasks
- **Social Learning**: Learning from other robots and humans
