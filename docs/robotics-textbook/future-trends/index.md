# Future Trends and Research Frontiers

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Identify emerging technologies that will shape the future of humanoid robotics
- Understand current research frontiers and unresolved challenges
- Analyze the potential impact of advanced materials and manufacturing techniques
- Evaluate research directions in collective and swarm robotics
- Assess the prospects for long-term autonomy and self-improving systems
